# Policies

## Make Up

Team Atomica offers a 7-Day Make-up Swim Policy for any swims you need to miss due to work, travel or sickness. This means if you miss one of your regular swims, you are welcome to swim at almost any other Team Atomica Spring 2026 Masters Swim that has space within 7 days. This means in the case of planned travel you can potentially make-up your swim before you leave as long as it is within 7 days.

### New 2006 Make Up

NEW THIS SEASON: Please email [adminTEAM@teamatomica.com](adminTEAM@teamatomica.com) to arrange a make-up swim. Note that Make-up swim requests are NOT processed on the weekends or after hours, so please make sure to get your request(s) in with enough time during Mon-Fri business hours for you to receive an answer back.

### New 2026 Plan Absence

NEW THIS SEASON: Please notify [adminTEAM@teamatomica.com](adminTEAM@teamatomica.com) in advance any dates you know you will be away so we can potentially offer your spot(s) to another swimmer, this is especially important if you swim at a session that is Full and this is how we can potentially accommodate you when you are requesting a Make-up swim.

### Weather & Other Cancellations

Weather & Other Cancellations out of Team Atomica's Control
(ie Labour Strikes, pool staff not showing up etc.) In the case of the first such cancellation within a Session, you will be provided with a 7-Day Make-up swim only at another Team Atomica available session. In the case of further cancellations out of Team Atomica‘s control, Team Atomica reserves the right to provide a CREDIT only or Session extension as compensation. In the case of an extended pool closure of 3+ consecutive weeks, REFUNDS will be Issued.

### Other Session Terms

Can be found near the bottom of our Spring 2026 Masters Swimming page on the Team Atomica website at: [https://teamatomica.com/2026-spring-masters/](https://teamatomica.com/2026-spring-masters/)  please take a moment to familiarize yourself with them.
